Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

1. What is CARDLORD?

CARDLORD is an online flashcard learning platform that helps users study and memorize vocabulary, concepts, and more through interactive flashcards.

2. How do I use CARDLORD?

Simply register for an account, create or browse flashcard sets, and start learning by flipping through the cards.

3. Is CARDLORD free to use?

Yes, CARDLORD offers free access to basic flashcard sets. However, there may be premium features available for upgraded accounts.

Account & Login

4. How do I create an account?

Click on the "Register" button, fill in your details, and follow the instructions to set up your account.

5. I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password." Follow the steps to reset your password via email.

6. Can I change my username?

Currently, usernames cannot be changed. However, you can create a new account if needed.

Flashcards & Study Sets

7. How do I create a flashcard set?

After logging in, click the “+” icon or navigate to the dashboard to create a new flashcard set by adding words and definitions.

8. Can I share my flashcard sets with others?

Yes! You can share your sets with friends by sending them a link or making them public.

9. How do I delete or edit a flashcard set?

Go to your dashboard, select the flashcard set you want to modify, and choose the edit or delete option.

Technical Support

10. The website is not working properly. What should I do?

Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the problem persists, contact our support team via the "Report a Problem" link.

11. How do I contact customer support?

You can reach out to our support team through the "Contact Support" link at the bottom of the website.
